# Intelligent Construction Invoice Automation

A portfolio case study demonstrating AI-assisted invoice pre-screening for a fictional construction company, Smith Construction. This is not a client deployment.

## The problem

In this hypothetical scenario, Smith Construction’s Accounts Payable team spends significant time manually checking whether construction invoices are complete and reasonable before entering them into the accounting system. The process introduces delays, inconsistency, and the risk of processing problematic invoices.

## The solution

The workflow classifies invoices as:

- **Clean:** No issues flagged by the defined pre-screening checks; the invoice proceeds to the AP team’s standard processing and approval workflow.
- **Needs Review:** Potential issues are flagged with reasons for the AP team to investigate.

The project uses document data extraction, AI-assisted analysis, and business rules to support invoice pre-screening. Human reviewers remain responsible for investigating exceptions and approving payments.
